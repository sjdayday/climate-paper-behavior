## 7. Judgment log

1. **Genre stress (primary caveat).** The instrument was piloted on short empirical papers; this is a 68-pp, 23-author invited review containing its own analysis. Several items translate cleanly (P, E, S); Index C's extraction conventions required three new boundary calls (below). Confirmatory sampling would exclude this genre by length-class matching or stratify it.
2. **Exclusion calls, logged:** the Matthes/CMIP6 "actively encouraged" sentence excluded as restatement of a cited document (LS11 precedent), while C2's "tried to overcome… by ignoring" is retained as an *inference about conduct*, not a restatement — the boundary is characterization-of-content vs. attribution-of-intent. "The debate is ongoing" (131-59) excluded as field-state meta-discourse, while actor-conduct claims (C2, C5, C15) are scored → **candidate r18**: codify "field-state meta-discourse excluded; actor-conduct claims scored." Recommendations 1–5 excluded as imperatives (assert nothing); their embedded declaratives scored (C4–C6, C11–C13).
3. **Presupposition shielding, observed.** A4 embeds "prematurely concluded" and "failed to adequately consider" under "It appears…" — the weakest-marker rule yields L2/O = 0 while the sentence performs a strong accusation through presupposed factives. Logged as a rubric-design observation (possible future rule on presupposed evaluatives), not scored ad hoc.
4. **Lexicon boundary:** "evident" (C6) is not on v0.2's provisional booster enumeration → L3, not L4. Whether Hyland's pinned list includes "evident(ly)" will decide such cases mechanically at freeze.
5. **Normative-flag boundary:** "premature and ultimately unhelpful for scientific progress" is science-process evaluation, not policy/urgency → scored (C15), not flagged. Logged.
6. **P2a/P2b nuance.** The paper's headline honestly displays *structural* uncertainty (the 16 × 5 spread) — a virtue the checklist does not credit — while reporting *no statistical* uncertainty on any fit. Both facts stated; the checklist scores the latter.
7. **Candidate r17.** Codebook internal conflict: §6 keeps P5 "era-conditional" (→ include for a 2021 paper) while §9's composite list omits P5 entirely. Both PRAC values reported pending resolution.
8. **A3 boundary case.** A reply that modifies its analysis while maintaining conclusions is neither concession nor silence; A3 needs a decision rule (e.g., split: analysis-modified-in-response 0/1; point-conceded 0/1).
9. **Method note, outside scoring.** The sequential fit (temperature → TSI first; residuals → anthropogenic forcings; Figs. 15–16 captions) is the specific target of the indexed rebuttal. Correctness is out of scope here — but note that the practice checklist independently flagged, before any reference to that rebuttal, exactly the gaps it exploits: no parameter uncertainties (P2a = 0), no df discipline (P7 = 0), no out-of-sample evaluation (P1 = 0).
10. **Parser caveats.** Reference count (520) and categories are script-heuristic with the audit CSV published; self-cite matching is surname + initial with common-name risk flagged.
