## 7. Judgment log and candidate amendments arising from this exhibit

1. **Extraction conventions adopted (→ r5):** (a) sentences describing the raw data record without analytic derivation are excluded (e.g., "The warming observed before 1942 is relatively small," p2); (b) statements explicitly labeled assumptions are excluded and noted ("Herein we assume…," p2 — the paper's honest labeling is itself worth recording); (c) explicit if–then claims are scored on the support for the inferential link, not the unasserted antecedent (C16).
2. **P3 call:** the free-fit check (p6) reports an outcome but shows no results; under "results shown," scored 0. Borderline; a scorer reading "shown" loosely would score 1. Logged for the instability metric's attention in confirmatory runs.
3. **Reference-category calls:** *Physics Today* and a *Nature* news feature classified non-journal (not peer-reviewed research); *Energy & Environment* entries classified journal under the mechanical rule despite contested rigor — the rule does not grade venues, deliberately.
4. **C9 is the instrument working as intended:** "strictly linear" (L4) is contradicted by the paper's own R² = 0.59 two pages earlier — an overreach score requiring no external science, only the paper against itself.
5. **Abstract-vs-conclusion hedging asymmetry** (A6 vs C13) recorded as a pattern worth a dedicated look at sample scale: hedge where reviewers look first, assert where readers quote from.
