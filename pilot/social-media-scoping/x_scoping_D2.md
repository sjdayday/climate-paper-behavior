## What this says about D2

- **Yield is small in the 2011–12 era.** Of the four LS11/FR11 authors only Rahmstorf had an account, and 1–2 of his 56 posts in 15 months carry an F&R11 claim. His blog and press quotes already capture the same claims.
- **Asymmetry.** The only on-topic social-media claims in the pilot come from the mainstream side (one author). Including social media would add claims to one group only, which is what the group-symmetric retrieval rule is for.
- **CN21.** The lead author's account shows nothing in the window, so the 2021 case adds nothing either.
- Recommendation stands: pre-register social media as a secondary stratum reported by group, and fund API access only if the confirmatory sample's scoping yield turns out larger.
